About

Associate Professor in Information Science and Engineering, teaching computer architecture, databases, networks and parallel computing.

Teacher, mentor and learner

I have more than 15 years of experience in teaching and research across computer science and electronics, from Digital Design and Computer Organization to Database Management Systems, Computer Networks and Parallel Computing. My research focuses on image processing, deep learning and machine learning. I share my class notes, lab manuals and question banks openly so that students can revise at their own pace.
